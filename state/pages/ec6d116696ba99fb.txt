1st Dusseldorf Breast Meeting
Call for Abstracts 2027
Present your case or study at the 1st Dusseldorf Breast Meeting. Every participant can submit — the best contributions are presented on stage, all others as e-posters.
Submission open
Until January 30, 2027, 11:59 p.m. CET
Submit abstract ↗ Submission runs on the DBM congress platform at rokits.ai. Sign in or register there and the abstract tool opens right away.
How it works
01 Every participant of the Dusseldorf Breast Meeting can submit an abstract — several, if they wish.
02 Submissions should cover interesting cases or studies in breast surgery.
03 Following a standardized review by the Scientific Committee, the best contributions are accepted and presented by their first authors in a dedicated session.
04 In this session the presenters have five minutes for their case or study, followed by two minutes of discussion with the chairs and the audience.
05 All other contributions are exhibited as e-posters, subject to the Scientific Committee’s check of basic scientific standards.
06 Everyone with an e-poster also gives a five-minute talk on it in the poster sessions.
What to submit
The title of the work
The type of contribution: a case (case report or case series) or a study
All authors in the order of the author line, with the institutions they belong to — exactly one person presents as first author
A statement on ethics approval: positive vote available, not required, or requested
The abstract as a Word file (.docx) and as a PDF, up to 20 MB each
Key dates
Submission open Since October 19, 2026
Deadline January 30, 2027, 11:59 p.m. CET
Congress April 9–10, 2027, Düsseldorf
How abstracts are reviewed
The Scientific Committee scores every abstract on six criteria, from 1 to 10 points each. The level of evidence is classified by the Oxford CEBM Levels of Evidence 2011.
Relevance for breast surgery
Scientific methodology
Level of evidence
Originality
Clinical impact
Presentation
Basic scientific standards — anonymization, consent, ethics and honest reporting — apply to every contribution. A work that does not meet them is not exhibited as an e-poster either.
Ready to submit?
Drafts can be saved and edited until the deadline, and a submission can be withdrawn.
