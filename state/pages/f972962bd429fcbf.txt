Cape Town 2025
Attendees
1
+
The 3rd ICOPLAST World Congress, took place from 17 to 19 September 2025 at the CTICC in Cape Town, South Africa. It was a great success, attended by over 500 surgeons, registrars, students, and industry partners from more than 50 countries. The excellent scientific program featured masterclasses and presentations by world-renowned plastic and reconstructive surgeons from across the globe. Delegates also had the opportunity to network and enjoy the vibrant social program that complemented the scientific sessions.
We sincerely thank, the Association of Plastic, Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgeons of Southern Africa (APRASSA) for graciously hosting a very memorable and successful congress.
