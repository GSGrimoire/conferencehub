GENERAL INFORMATION
INSPIRING, INNOVATING, AND INTEGRATING
The second edition of the iCALS Meeting will take place in Rome.
This unique international event has been designed to showcase the latest innovations and state-of-the-art developments in lymphatic surgery, bringing together leading experts from around the world.
The management of lymphatic disorders requires a multidisciplinary and holistic approach. For this reason, iCALS will provide a unique opportunity for lymphatic surgeons to meet and collaborate with specialists from various medical disciplines, as well as physiotherapists, through dedicated scientific sessions and hands-on workshops.
In addition, iCALS will offer dedicated opportunities for the Rising Stars of lymphatic surgery, who will have the chance to present their research and share the stage with internationally renowned experts.

Scientific sessions featuring original research related to the Congress topics will be held throughout the meeting.

Abstract submission deadline: November 15, 2026
